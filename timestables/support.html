It doesn't hear me

  • Open the iPhone Settings app → Times Tables and make sure Microphone and Speech Recognition are on.
  • In the game, tap ⚙️ → Test the microphone and say a number.
  • In the car, put the phone where it can hear everyone, and speak up a little over road noise.

The voice is too fast, too slow or too quiet

  • Tap ⚙️ → Game voice, then drag the 🐢 / 🐇 slider. Normal resets it.
  • Pick a different voice from the same menu; choosing one plays a sample.
  • Turn up the volume. The game uses normal media volume, so it also plays over car Bluetooth.

Play without talking

Choose Tap it on the home screen to answer with a keypad.

Players

  • Tap someone's picture on the home screen to put them in or out of today's game (up to four at once). Their progress is always kept.
  • Press and hold a picture to edit or delete a player.

Progress and the leaderboard

  • Tap the 📊 button on the home screen to see "Who knows what", a grid of every fact from needs-practice to automatic.
  • The leaderboard on the home screen shows this week and all time. Progress is kept on each device and isn't shared between devices.
