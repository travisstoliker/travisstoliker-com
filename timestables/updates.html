What's new

You asked, we answered!

Updates based on your feedback. Families and testers told us what would make car rides better, and here's what we built.

Coming in 1.7

"My kid has to shout the answers in the car."

🔇 A road-noise filter. While the game listens, it filters out the hum of the road and raises quiet voices, so normal talking works. It's on by default, in Settings under Microphone.

New in 1.6

"The phone can't hear everyone in the car."

🚗 Listen through the car's microphone. Turn it on in Settings, under Microphone, and the game listens through CarPlay or the car's Bluetooth microphone instead of the phone.

New in 1.6

"Starting a game while driving is hard."

🎙️ "Hey Siri, start Times Tables." Say it, or press the CarPlay Siri button and say it, and a round starts for the players picked last time.

New in 1.5

"Our kids have their own iPads with different Apple IDs."

📲 Add another device with a short code. Tap Add another device on a device that saves your family online, then Join with a code on the other one. Nobody signs in there, and same-named players become one.

New in 1.5

"Can it keep track of how many days we practice?"

🔥 Day streaks. See how many days in a row you've practiced, and hear it at the start of each day's first round.

New in 1.4

"We forget to practice."

⏰ Daily reminders (optional). Pick a time in Settings, and the game cheers on your streaks and the new facts each player learns.

New in 1.3

Joining the world leaderboard took too many steps.

🌎 One screen to join. Tap Join the world leaderboard on the World tab, or say yes when the game asks after a round. A parent signs in if needed, switches on each player and taps Save. A grown-up confirms with Face ID, a passcode or a fingerprint before anyone goes on.

New in 1.2

"Can it mix them all up in one round?"

🔀 Mix mode. Times, divide, plus and minus questions in the same round, with its own leaderboard. Each answer still trains that kind of fact, so the coach keeps learning what each player knows.

New in 1.2

"Can we see how we stack up against other families?"

🌎 World leaderboard. A parent can turn it on for each player. It shows only a first name and last initial (like "Lane S."), their animal and their points, for each mode, this week and all time. Names are checked, anyone can report one, and you can turn it off any time.

Fixed in 1.1

"I typed the answer before the question finished and it didn't count."

Answers typed early now count in Tap it, and two-digit answers always work, even if you start before the timer bar moves.

Fixed in 1.1

"It said my fastest answer took 0.0 seconds."

Answer before the question ends and it now says "before the question was even finished!" instead of 0.0s.

New in 1.1

"Can we practice division, adding and subtracting too?"

÷ Divide, + Plus and − Minus. Each mode has its own coach, leaderboard and progress chart, from single digits up to two-digit numbers with carrying and borrowing.

New

"Can I play on my school Chromebook?"

Play in any browser, on a Chromebook or computer, plus a full Android app. A parent can save the family's scores online so they match on every device.

Everything in 1.7

Waiting for Apple's review

Since 1.6. For iPhone and iPad. Sent to Apple on November 14, 2026.

  • 🔇 Filter out road noise: a switch in Settings, under Microphone, on by default. While the game listens on the device's own microphone, it filters out steady background noise and raises quiet voices. Turn it off if it ever hears you worse.
  • "Whoa, that was fast!" replaces "Whoa, speedy!", which sometimes got cut off. Short cheers are now made twice and the fuller one kept, so they always play in full.
  • ⭐ Rate the app: after a great round, Apple's rating box may appear. It never interrupts a game.

Everything in 1.6

Live on the App Store

Since 1.5. Out now: get it on the App Store for iPhone and iPad.

  • 🚗 Listen through the car's microphone: a switch in Settings, under Microphone. When the phone is connected to CarPlay or a car's Bluetooth, the game listens through the car's microphone. It's off unless you turn it on, because the game's voice may sound more like a phone call.
  • 🎙️ "Hey Siri, start Times Tables": opens the game and starts a round for the players picked last time. It also works from the CarPlay Siri button. The phone needs to be unlocked.

Everything in 1.5

On the App Store before 1.6

Since 1.4. Released November 12, 2026.

  • 📲 Add another device with a short code: on a device that already saves your family online, open Settings and tap Add another device. On the other iPhone or iPad, tap Join with a code. Nobody signs in there, and it works whatever Apple ID that device uses.
  • Players combine: a player with the same name on both devices becomes one player, and nothing is erased.
  • 🔥 Day streaks: see how many days in a row you've practiced, and hear it at the start of each day's first round.

Everything in 1.4

On the App Store before 1.5

Since 1.3. Released November 9, 2026.

  • ⏰ Daily practice reminders (optional): pick a time in Settings, and we'll cheer on your streaks and the new facts each player learns.
  • A quick welcome tour for new families.
  • About half the download size, with the same natural voice, and saved voice lines take far less space on your phone.
  • 🌎 World leaderboard: a player who just joined shows up right away, and you can block a family from your board.

Everything in 1.3

On the App Store before 1.4

Since 1.2. Also live in your browser, and the Android test has it too.

  • Join the world leaderboard button on the leaderboard's 🌎 World tab, even before anyone has signed in.
  • One screen for everything: a parent signs in if needed, then a switch and a last initial for each player, with a preview like "Shows as Lane S.", and one Save.
  • A one-time question after a round: "Show Lane on the world leaderboard?" Yes opens that screen with Lane already switched on; No thanks stops asking.
  • The family's initial fills in when you switch on another player.
  • A grown-up confirms before a player goes on: Face ID or the passcode on iPhone and iPad, a fingerprint, face or screen lock on Android, a confirm box on the web. Turning a player off never asks.
  • Fixed: a player who was on the world leaderboard could look switched off on a phone and stop updating. The game now checks the leaderboard itself.

Everything in 1.2

On the App Store before 1.3

Since 1.1. Released October 16, 2026.

  • 🔀 Mix mode: all four kinds of questions in one round, with its own 🔀 leaderboard tab.
  • 🌎 World leaderboard (optional): off until a parent turns it on for a player, from the player's settings or when adding a new player.
  • Only "Lane S." style names: first name and last initial, letters only, checked against a list of words that aren't allowed.
  • Report button on every world leaderboard row. Names that aren't OK are taken down for good.
  • Turn it off any time: the row disappears right away, and deleting a player or the account removes it too.
  • Mix rounds sync with your family account like every other mode, and are deleted with it.
  • Smoother sign-in on the web: signing in during start-up no longer gets lost.

Everything in 1.1

On the App Store before 1.2

Since 1.0, the first version.

  • ÷ Divide, + Plus and − Minus modes, each with its own coach, leaderboard and "Who knows what" chart.
  • Tap it fix: answers typed while the question is still being read count, and two-digit answers always work.
  • No more "0.0s": an early answer says "before the question was even finished!"
  • Try again after a misheard answer: saying "repeat" right after a miss takes it back once.
  • Pause and Repeat keep the answer clock going instead of restarting it.
  • Points come only from answer speed, up to 15 per question, so it's fair for everyone.
  • A slightly tougher coach: it now aims for about 80% right, hard enough to learn and easy enough to keep winning.
  • Save scores online (optional): one parent signs in with Apple or Google and the whole family's players and scores show up on every device and the website.
  • Delete your account in the app, any time, from Settings.
  • iCloud: your own iPhones and iPads share players and progress automatically.
  • Leaderboard opens on All time, and weeks start on Monday.
  • A new 6×7 app icon, plus privacy and support links in Settings.
  • Play anywhere: the same game in a web browser and on Android, with Sunny's voice.
