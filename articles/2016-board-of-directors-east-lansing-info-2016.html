Board of Directors, East Lansing Info (2016)

May 03, 2016

I am proud to announce that I have joined the Board of Directors of East Lansing Info. ELi is a non-profit citizen-run local news cooperative that covers East Lansing. I've been a longtime reader and donor to the organization. If you're interested in East Lansing news you should consider liking their Facebook page or subscribing to their email list.
