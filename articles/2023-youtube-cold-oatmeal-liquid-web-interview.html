What is Liquid Web? How Did You Market LiquidWeb? — Travis Stoliker on Cold Oatmeal

2023
Source · YouTube / Cold Oatmeal

Travis Stoliker appeared on the Cold Oatmeal podcast to discuss his years building and marketing Liquid Web — the Lansing-based managed hosting company that grew from 10 employees when Travis joined around 2006 to over 480 employees and approximately $80 million in annual recurring revenue by the time of its $224 million sale to Madison Dearborn Partners in 2015.

Clips from the interview were published on Travis Stoliker's Year of the Opposite YouTube channel. The interview covers what managed hosting is and why it's different from commodity hosting, the marketing and sales philosophy that drove Liquid Web's national recognition, and the company's evolution from a small Lansing shop into one of the largest managed hosting providers in the country.

A second clip from the same Cold Oatmeal appearance — "What did you do after selling Liquid Web for $224 million?" — covers Stoliker's life after the 2015 exit, including his investment in Saddleback BBQ and his ongoing entrepreneurial work. That clip is at youtube.com/watch?v=xVkd1IfEY6w.
