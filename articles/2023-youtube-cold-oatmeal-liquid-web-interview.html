What is Liquid Web? How Did You Market LiquidWeb? — Travis Stoliker on Cold Oatmeal

Published: 2023  ·  Source: YouTube / Year of the Opposite

Travis Stoliker appeared on the Cold Oatmeal podcast to discuss his years building and marketing Liquid Web, one of Michigan's most successful tech companies. The interview covers how Liquid Web grew from a small hosting company to a national leader, and the marketing and sales strategies that drove that growth.

Clips from the full interview were published on Travis Stoliker's Year of the Opposite YouTube channel (Year Of The Opposite, 13 subscribers).

In the interview, Stoliker discusses: — What Liquid Web is and what makes managed hosting different — How he approached marketing and sales for a technical product — The early days of building the company and the team — What led to the eventual $224 million sale in 2015

A second clip from the same Cold Oatmeal appearance was also published: "What did you do after selling Liquid Web for $224 million?" (youtube.com/watch?v=xVkd1IfEY6w), covering Stoliker's life after the exit — including investing in Saddleback BBQ and his ongoing entrepreneurial work.
