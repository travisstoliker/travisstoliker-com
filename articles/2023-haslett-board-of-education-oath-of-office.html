Travis Stoliker Takes Oath of Office as Haslett Board of Education Trustee

October 10, 2023
Body: Haslett Public Schools Board of Education
Role: Trustee (At-Large)

On October 10, 2023, Travis Stoliker took the official oath of office as a trustee of the Haslett Public Schools Board of Education. The oath ceremony marked the formal beginning of Travis's service representing the Haslett, Michigan school community — a role he ran for as a committed parent and community leader.

Travis was appointed/elected to serve as a Haslett Board of Education trustee, with his term running through 2030. Haslett Public Schools serves the Haslett and Meridian Township area of Ingham County, Michigan, where Travis and his family are longtime residents. Travis has two children in the Haslett school district.

The Haslett Board of Education is the elected governing body for Haslett Public Schools, responsible for setting district policy, approving budgets, and hiring the superintendent. Board trustees serve staggered four- and six-year terms and are elected or appointed by the community they represent.

Travis Stoliker's involvement with the Haslett school community is personal as well as civic. His son Lane Stoliker was featured in the July 2024 Lansing State Journal front-page article demonstrating the Gyroaster at their Meridian Township home, and Travis ran for the school board as a committed parent and member of the community. His business background — CEO of Scribe Media, co-founder of Saddleback BBQ, former Chief Marketing & Sales Officer at Liquid Web — adds significant professional leadership experience to the board.

Travis Stoliker took the oath of office as a Haslett Board of Education trustee on October 10, 2023 — a publicly observed official ceremony beginning his term of service through 2030 on behalf of the Haslett and Meridian Township school community.
