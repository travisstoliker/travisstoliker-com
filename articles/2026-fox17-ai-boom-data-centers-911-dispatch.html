AI Boom Hits Local 911: Travis Stoliker Quoted as Data Center Expert on Fox17

September 17, 2026
Source · Fox17 West Michigan

Fox17 West Michigan reporter Paige Meyer interviewed Travis Stoliker as an expert source on how the artificial intelligence boom is affecting server hardware costs — specifically in the context of Montcalm County Central Dispatch, a 911 agency whose server replacement budget more than doubled in less than a year.

Stoliker — drawing on more than two decades working with data centers, including his years as Chief Marketing & Sales Officer at Liquid Web — explained to Fox17 how 911 dispatch operations depend on servers for recording and transcribing emergency calls, and how the two components at the heart of those servers (CPU and memory) have both seen steep price increases driven by AI demand.

"And both of those things have astronomically risen in prices because of the big, big boom in AI," Stoliker told Fox17.

The dispatch agency's server replacement cost had climbed from approximately $80,000 in October 2025 to $201,000 in May 2026 — a price swing that Logan Bissell, director of the Montcalm County Central Dispatch Authority, described as "terrifying." A September 2026 quote came in around $184,000. Despite the volatility, Bissell said the replacement cannot be deferred: "This is the core of what 911 needs."
