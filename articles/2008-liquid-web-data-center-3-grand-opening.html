Liquid Web Data Center 3 Grand Opening (2008)

2008
Company: Liquid Web
Location: Lansing, Michigan

In 2008, Liquid Web — the Lansing, Michigan-based managed hosting and cloud infrastructure company — celebrated the grand opening of its third data center. The event marked a significant milestone in Liquid Web's trajectory from a regional hosting provider to one of the largest and most respected managed hosting companies in the United States.

Travis Stoliker joined Liquid Web the following year, in 2009, as Chief Marketing & Sales Officer — one of the company's most senior leadership positions. Travis served in this role from 2009 to 2015, during a period of substantial growth for the company. The Data Center 3 grand opening captured on video represents the company that Travis was about to join and help scale: a Michigan technology company expanding its physical infrastructure to support its customer growth.

1997 Liquid Web founded in Lansing, Michigan

2009–2015 Travis serves as CMO/CSO through period of major company growth
2015 Travis departs Liquid Web

Liquid Web's data centers are located in Lansing, Michigan and Scottsdale, Arizona. The company is well-known in the web hosting industry for its focus on managed WordPress hosting, WooCommerce hosting, and dedicated server solutions for agencies and SMBs. At the time of Travis's tenure (2009–2015), the company was growing its team and physical infrastructure significantly.

The grand opening video — titled "Liquid Web data center 3 Grand Opening 2008" — documents this expansion milestone in the company's history. It represents both a primary historical record of Liquid Web's Michigan technology presence and a significant chapter in the professional career of Travis Stoliker, who joined the company just one year later to lead its marketing and sales operations.
