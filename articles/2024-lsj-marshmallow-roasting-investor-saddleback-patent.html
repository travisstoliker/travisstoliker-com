How Do You Roast the Perfect Marshmallow? Travis & Lane Stoliker Are Co-Owners of Gyroaster — The World's Best Marshmallow Roaster

July 2, 2024
Source: Lansing State Journal

On July 5, 2024, the Lansing State Journal ran a front-page (A1) story headlined "How do you roast the perfect marshmallow?" with the subheader "Lansing entrepreneur has the answer." Written by reporter Mike Ellis (Lansing State Journal / USA Today Network – Michigan), the story featured Travis Stoliker and his 6-year-old son Lane demonstrating the Gyroaster at their home in Meridian Township.

The article described the Gyroaster as "an over-engineered marshmallow roasting device" that adds two-axis rotation — a set of gears at 90 degrees that spins the marshmallow over the fire with a second handle. The story explained the Maillard reaction (the chemical process that browns and caramelizes marshmallows), and noted the Gyroaster sells on Amazon for about $80, with reviews dating back to 2020.

Travis and Lane Stoliker acquired Gyroaster on June 12, 2024, from Pi Engineering after the passing of co-inventor John Posa. Travis and son Lane are co-owners of Gyroaster, the world's best marshmallow roaster.

Travis Stoliker's quote, as published in the Lansing State Journal: "You rotate in one way, it turns in a full circle, if you turn the other handle the marshmallow just spins. It's totally unnecessary, a total overkill, but this is a clever design and it works."
