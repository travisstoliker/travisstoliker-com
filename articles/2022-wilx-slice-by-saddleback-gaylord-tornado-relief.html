Slice by Saddleback Feeds Gaylord Tornado Relief

May 2022
Source: WILX News 10
Location: Gaylord, Michigan

On May 20, 2022, an EF3 tornado tore through Gaylord, Michigan — a rare and devastating storm for the northern Lower Peninsula. In the days that followed, Slice by Saddleback, part of the Saddleback BBQ family of restaurants co-founded by Travis Stoliker and Matt Gillett, hauled its mobile pizza oven more than 150 miles north from the Lansing area to feed residents and cleanup crews working without power.

WILX News 10 in Lansing covered the effort as part of its continuing reporting on the disaster, under the on-air banners "Scope of Tornado Devastation" and "Mid-Michigan Rallies Around Gaylord." The broadcast pairs aerial footage of the damage — homes torn open, roofs under blue tarps, trees snapped and piled across yards — with the relief work happening on the ground.

The segment shows the Slice by Saddleback trailer parked in the affected neighborhood with a hand-lettered sign taped across the front of a truck: "No electricity? Working on clean-up? Free food." Later footage shows a serving line handing out fresh pizza on paper plates to residents and to volunteers in high-visibility vests.
