Saddleback BBQ Owners Paid Off Mason Schools' Lunch Debt

March 30, 2021
Source: Lansing State Journal

Archive note: The original article URL is no longer accessible (the source site returned a 404 error as of September 2026). This page preserves what is known about this article from search engine indexing and public records. This is precisely the scenario this archive was built to address.

Travis Stoliker and his Saddleback BBQ co-owner Matthew Gillett paid off the school lunch debt for Mason Public Schools in March 2021. The story was covered by the Lansing State Journal on March 30, 2021. Per the article snippet, over $6,300 in lunch debt was paid off. This was part of a pattern of community giving by Stoliker and Gillett during and after the COVID-19 pandemic — they had previously helped Coach's Pub & Grill, Mitten Raised bakery, and various community members in need. They later also paid off lunch debt at other Lansing-area school districts.
