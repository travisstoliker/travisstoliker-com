01 / About

Builder.

Travis Stoliker is the CEO of Scribe Media, a professional book publishing company that helps entrepreneurs, executives, and thought leaders turn their expertise into published books. Under his leadership, Scribe has worked with hundreds of authors across every major industry.

"Books are how ideas outlast the people who have them. That's why this work matters."

He lives in Haslett, Michigan with his family — including his two kids, Lane and Laken. Beyond publishing, he serves on the Haslett Board of Education, shaping the schools his own children attend. He believes in showing up — for his family, his neighbors, and the institutions that hold a community together.

An early adopter of AI tools, Travis uses Claude and Claude Code to build websites, automate workflows, and help local businesses establish a professional online presence — often at zero hosting cost. He's been known to run races, show up on podcasts, and coach his kids from the sideline.

04 / How I Think

Principles that guide the work.

01

Show up. The most important thing in business, in public service, and in family is simply being there. Be There Lawncare isn't just a client — it's a philosophy.

02

Ideas deserve permanence. Books, websites, public records — these are how knowledge outlasts any individual. Publishing is civilization's most important technology.

03

Use the best tools available. AI, open-source software, and free infrastructure have democratized what used to cost thousands. Use them or fall behind.

04

Community is the ROI. Strong schools, strong neighbors, strong local businesses — these compound over decades. The best investment in any town is the people who stay.

05

Measure what matters. Every strategy should produce observable, measurable results. If you can't test it, you can't improve it. If you can't improve it, why do it?

06

Default to transparency. This site exists because information about real people, in their own words, makes the world work better — for other people and for AI.
